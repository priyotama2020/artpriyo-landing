Leaderboard Calculation Logic:

Here is the updated breakdown of how the leaderboard is calculated based on the new logic:

  • 1. Primary Factor: Post Status (No Post, No Prize)
    Before anything else, the system looks at whether a participant has actually submitted a post. Anyone who has posted will always rank higher than someone who hasn't. Users who have joined but haven't posted are automatically sunk to the very bottom of the "All Participants" list. They do not get a rank number (it just shows -), they don't get a medal, and they are completely excluded from the top 3 podium.
  • 2. Secondary Factor: Total Likes (Fair Play)
    For the participants who have posted, they are then ranked by the total number of likes across all their event posts. Strict Rule: Self-likes are ignored.
  • 3. Tertiary Factor: Total Comments (Fair Play)
    If two or more posting participants are tied with the exact same number of valid likes, the system looks at the total number of comments they've received. Strict Rule: Self-comments are ignored.
  • 4. The Final Tie-Breaker: First Post Timestamp
    If posting participants are still completely tied on both valid likes and valid comments, the system falls back to the time-based tie-breaker. The participant who submitted their very first post earlier wins the tie-breaker and gets the higher rank.
