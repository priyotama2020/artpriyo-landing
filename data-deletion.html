Data Deletion Policy

How to request account and data deletion

Last updated: July 2026

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

ArtPriyo respects your right to privacy and gives you full control over your personal data. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, you have two options:

Option 1: In-App Deletion

  1. Open the ArtPriyo mobile app and log into your account.
  2. Navigate to your Profile.
  3. Tap the Settings icon.
  4. Select Account Details.
  5. Tap on Delete Account and follow the confirmation prompts.

Option 2: Web Request

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request data deletion by contacting our support team:

  • Send an email to priyotama2020@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account.
  • Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request".
  • Please allow up to 72 hours for processing.

What Data is Deleted?

Upon account deletion, the following data will be permanently removed from our active servers:

  • Your profile information (name, bio, profile picture)
  • Your uploaded artwork and competition entries
  • Your wallet data (please ensure you have withdrawn any balances before deleting)
  • Your social interactions (likes, comments)

Note: We may retain certain transaction records for legal and tax compliance purposes as required by law.
