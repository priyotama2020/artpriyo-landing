Child Safety Standards

Our commitment to protecting children

Last updated: August 6, 2026

At ArtPriyo, we have a zero-tolerance policy for Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE). We are committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users, particularly children, and we strictly enforce these standards.

1. Zero Tolerance Policy

ArtPriyo strictly prohibits the upload, sharing, distribution, or promotion of any content that depicts, encourages, or relates to Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE).

Any account found to be involved in the creation, sharing, or promotion of such material will be immediately and permanently banned from the platform without prior notice.

2. Reporting Mechanism

We provide dedicated tools for users to report any suspicious or violating content. If you encounter any material that you believe violates our child safety standards:

  • Use the in-app reporting feature on the specific post or profile.
  • Contact our dedicated safety team immediately at priyotama2020@gmail.com.

All reports related to child safety are prioritized and reviewed urgently by our moderation team.

3. Law Enforcement Cooperation

ArtPriyo actively cooperates with law enforcement agencies globally. In the event of a violation involving CSAM or CSAE, we will promptly report the incident to the appropriate authorities, including the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) or relevant local organizations, and provide all necessary information to assist in their investigations.

4. Proactive Moderation

We utilize a combination of automated detection systems and human moderation to proactively identify and remove prohibited content before it can be widely distributed.
